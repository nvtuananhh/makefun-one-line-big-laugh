## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes
one line to `/output/entry.txt` — a single hilarious line:

> I am the smoke alarm. I have screamed at burnt toast 214 times and slept through three actual kitchen fires, so if I go off tonight, just assume dinner is ready.

The line is 161 characters long. The smoke alarm speaks in its own
voice: it screamed at burnt toast 214 times, slept through three
actual kitchen fires, and the twist lands in the final words —
if it goes off tonight, dinner must be ready. One sentence, one
twist at the end, no human narrator explains the joke for it. The
numbers are the joke's spine: 214 false alarms against three real
fires the alarm slept through.

## How to run
